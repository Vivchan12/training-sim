A USEFUL PLACE TO START

Transformation starts with understanding,
not technology.

Before deciding what technology should do, we first understand what needs to improve, who is affected, how the work happens today, and what the technology can realistically contribute.

  1. 01Business

    What are we actually trying to make better?

    Start with the outcome, friction or opportunity that matters to the organisation. AI is useful when it improves something worth improving.

  2. 02People

    Who will use, trust or be affected by the change?

    A technically possible idea still has to make sense to the people doing the work, making the decisions, serving customers or carrying accountability.

  3. 03Workflow

    How does the work happen today, and what should change?

    AI often creates more value when the surrounding work, handoffs and decisions are redesigned rather than simply adding another tool to the existing process.

  4. 04Technology

    What can AI realistically contribute?

    We look at capability, data, constraints and failure modes in context. The goal is not to use the most advanced technology. It is to use the right capability for the work.

ADOPTION IN PRACTICE

Adoption requires more than training.

Training helps people understand a tool. Lasting adoption also depends on whether the new way of working makes sense in the job, the workflow, the leadership environment, and the everyday decisions people need to make.

Training
Adoption

01 / 06

  1. 01

    Understanding

    Why are we changing?

    People need to understand why the new way of working matters.

    Adoption becomes easier when the reason for the change is clear and connected to the work people already care about.

  2. 02

    Useful workflow

    Does it fit the work?

    The new capability has to fit the way work actually happens.

    If using AI creates more steps, unclear handoffs or extra friction, training alone will not make the behaviour stick.

  3. 03

    Confidence

    Can people use it well?

    People need enough confidence to use judgement around the technology.

    Confidence comes from practice, clear boundaries, feedback, and knowing when to trust, verify or escalate.

  4. 04

    Leadership support

    Is the change reinforced?

    Leaders reinforce which behaviours matter.

    People watch what leaders prioritise, reward and ask about. Adoption grows when the change is supported in everyday management.

  5. 05

    Feedback

    Can the system learn?

    The organisation needs a way to learn from real use.

    Feedback shows where the workflow, experience, guidance or technology needs to change after deployment.

  6. 06

    Capability

    Can the organisation sustain it?

    The organisation needs the capability to sustain the change.

    Ownership, governance, skills and operating routines help the new way of working remain useful after the initial launch.
